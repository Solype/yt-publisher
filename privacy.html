Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

yt-publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Data accessed

When you authorize yt-publisher with your Google account, it can access the YouTube data covered by the scopes you approve: your channel, your videos and their metadata, and comments on your videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage videos on your own channel, at your request. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

The OAuth tokens are stored only on your own computer, in a file readable by your user only. yt-publisher has no server and does not store, collect or send your data anywhere other than to Google's APIs.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

yt-publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke yt-publisher's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
