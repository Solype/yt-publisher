yt-publisher

yt-publisher is a personal command-line tool. It lets the owner of this project publish and manage videos on their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to the owner's channel.
  • Edits titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists of the owner's videos.
  • Reads and replies to comments on the owner's videos.

It runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
